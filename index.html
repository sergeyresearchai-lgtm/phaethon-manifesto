4. Sanctioned Risk Zones
- Voluntary "reality territories" without systemic safety nets:
- Scientific outposts
- Artistic communes
- Expeditionary projects
- Here, true failure — and true triumph — are permitted
5. The "Eternal Child" Program
- For those choosing passivity:
- A state of "blissful childhood"
- Limited societal influence
- Option to "awaken" through voluntary challenges

- System achieves self-regulation
- Humanity divides into:
* Seekers (20%)
